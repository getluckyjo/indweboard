  • What drives the ramp. 2,000 registered players at the start, growing 10.5% a month in year one, 8% in year two and 7% in year three, against 4% monthly churn. The Ernie partner network adds 200,000 players across the three years, arriving from month three as integrations go live.
  • What each player does. Roughly one paid insured swing a month. Entries are bought on the tee, one swing at a time — premium is collected on the same swing that creates the risk, which is what makes the cover underwritable.
  • Why the risk prices. On the modelled mix, premium is $4.08 an entry against an expected claim of $1.30 — a 31.9% loss ratio, or 51.9% combined after brokerage. Aces would have to occur 2.51 times more often than amateur odds predict before the cover stopped making money. Largest single exposure is capped at $100,000.
  • Currency. Converted at R18.50 to the dollar.

Why this matters more than the market size. Of the golfers who gave a reason for declining a quote, 46% said they were happy with their current broker. Nobody moves a decade-old relationship on a first call. The value of the list above is not that it reaches this audience once — it is that it gives Indwe a reason to be in front of them repeatedly, until the moment their circumstances change.

These are market-sizing estimates, not actuarial valuations, and not a conversion forecast. Range R550bn to R1.15trn, central case R850bn.

05 The commercial option

If the international route is pursued, one structure has been discussed. It is recorded here as an option under consideration, not a proposal on the table.

★ An option under consideration

For every rand of international sponsorship secured with Indwe’s help, Indwe’s annual South African sponsorship would reduce by the same amount — rand for rand.

  • What it would require. Introductions to international insurance partners. Indwe’s standing in the industry opens doors that Get Lucky’s does not.
  • What it would preserve. The South African partnership continues unchanged — the brand placement, the database, the app, the lead pipeline — at a reducing annual cost.
  • What it would add. The brokerage position set out in section 03 as the app scales into other markets.

Nothing here is agreed or contracted. It is set out so that the figures in the main report can be understood in terms of what they would depend on.
